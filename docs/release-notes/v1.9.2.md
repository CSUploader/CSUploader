## Highlights

**DropMB uploads work again — with an account.** DropMB stopped accepting uploads from anyone who is
not signed in, so every upload to it failed with "HTTP 403 … Forbidden resource". CSUploader now
treats DropMB as an account-only hoster and keeps the account signed in for you.

## What changed

- **DropMB needs an account.** Its **Anonymous** choice is gone from the upload wizard, and a file
  that was already queued as Anonymous now fails straight away with a message saying why, instead of
  being sent to be refused. Add a DropMB account in **Settings → Accounts**, using your username *or*
  your email address and your password. DropMB is not currently accepting new sign-ups, and its
  accounts come through Google, GitHub or Discord, so you may first need to set a password on your
  account on dropmb.com.
- **A signed-in upload no longer fails once the sign-in lapses.** The sign-in DropMB hands out is
  only good for 15 minutes, and DropMB turns away a lapsed one exactly as it turns away no sign-in at
  all — the same "403 Forbidden resource". CSUploader now signs in again with the saved password
  whenever that happens, once per account rather than once per file, and in the middle of a large
  file too, picking up the chunk where it stopped.
- **An email address works as the account name.** Anything containing an `@` is sent to DropMB as an
  email, which is how its own sign-in form treats it.
- **Accounts with two-factor sign-in say so.** CSUploader cannot complete a two-factor sign-in, and
  the message now says that instead of blaming the upload.
- **The "Kept for" column reads 100 days for DropMB** instead of Permanent. DropMB's own page says a
  share is deleted after 100 days without a visit, and each visit starts the 100 days again.

## What to know

This was written from DropMB's own published settings and source and from a refused upload we could
reproduce, but it has not been run with a real DropMB account, because the site is not taking new
ones. If a signed-in upload still fails, the error message now carries DropMB's own answer — please
send it along.

The Anonymous hoster count in the README drops from 40 to 39.
