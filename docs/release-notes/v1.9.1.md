## Highlights

**Switching tabs no longer resets the lists.** Coming back to a tab used to find its lists scrolled
back to the top with nothing selected, and on the Settings and Logs tabs unsorted as well. Now they
keep their sort, selection and scroll position, except the accounts list in Settings, which reloads
each time you open Settings.

## What changed

- **The lists keep their place when you switch tabs.** On Uploads, History and Logs, and in the
  proxy list in Settings, the sort, the selection and the scroll position are as you left them. Each
  visit to another tab also left hidden copies of the lists behind, which went on processing every
  change to them until the app closed (four for each visit to Logs); that no longer happens.
- **Settings → Accounts keeps its sort and the selected account.** It reloads its list each time you
  open Settings, so it still comes back scrolled to the top, and with only that one account selected
  if you had selected several.
- **The ▲/▼ priority buttons in Settings → Connection move a proxy straight away.** The new order
  was saved, but the list kept showing the old one until you left Settings and came back. If you
  were still editing a cell, ▲/▼ finish that edit first, as clicking another row does. If the list is
  sorted by a column, ▲/▼ put it back in priority order, as reordering the Uploads queue does.
- **The Uploads filter works while a rename or an Order number is still being typed.** Typing in the
  filter box with one of those edits open did nothing until you confirmed the edit. Now the edit is
  finished first, keeping what you typed, and the filter applies.

## What did not change

How the tabs and lists look is unchanged. Only what a list remembers when you switch tabs, and the
fixes above, are new.
