## Highlights

**Seven new file hosters** — CSUploader now uploads to **53**, up from 46. Five of them need no
account at all, bringing the no-login total to **twenty-five**.

Also in this release: the upload wizard stops sending files a host will refuse. It now deselects
them before you press Next, and — new since 1.2.0 — it explains itself when a hoster it was told to
use will not actually receive anything.

## New file hosters

**No account needed** — pick "Anonymous" in the wizard:

- **Qu.ax** — 256 MiB, and the only one of these whose files can be **permanent**. Its own form
  defaults to 30 days; CSUploader asks for permanent instead. ⚠ It runs an **allowlist**: `.rar`,
  `.zip` and `.7z` pass, `.r00`, `.sfv` and `.nfo` do not, so a classic multi-part set is only
  half-accepted — the wizard now unticks the parts it won't take.
- **Upload.ee** — 100 MB anonymous, kept 50 days after the last download. With an account: **200 MB
  and 120 days**. ⚠ It inspects *inside* archives (200 MB per unpacked file, 400 MB total, and no
  more than fivefold expansion above 50 MB), which nothing else here does.
- **Temp.sh** — 4 GB, the largest of these. ⚠ Files **expire after 3 days**.
- **Litterbox** — 1 GB, catbox.moe's temporary sibling: five times the size, in exchange for
  permanence. ⚠ **72 hours** is the longest retention it offers, and that is what CSUploader asks
  for — a link posted on Friday is gone by Monday.
- **TmpFiles** — 100 MiB. ⚠ Its default retention is **one hour**; CSUploader sends the documented
  maximum (48 hours) instead.

**With an account:**

- **EliteFile** — no per-file cap, 488 GB of storage on the free tier. It publishes links on a
  different domain than the one you upload to, which CSUploader now follows.
- **Easybytez** — 200 MB per file, 10 GB of storage on a free account. Its page renders a guest
  upload form, but the server refuses guest uploads, so an account is required.

## Upload wizard

- **Files a host would refuse are deselected before you press Next**, with a sentence naming them.
  Three hosts enforce file-type rules (qu.ax's allowlist, Uploadrar's video blocklist, filedot.to's
  image blocklist) and two of them only enforce theirs *after* the whole file has transferred.
- **A hoster that will receive nothing now says why.** Picking a hoster whose account is switched off
  in Settings → Accounts, or whose last check failed, used to produce a summary reading "16 files
  won't be uploaded to any hoster" — the files listed, the reason never given. The hoster step now
  explains it where the choice is made, and blocks Next when nothing can upload.
- **Accounts switched off in Settings → Accounts are no longer offered** in the wizard's account
  picker. They were never usable; listing them only invited picking one.

## Fixes

- **EliteFile** saved the word "Settings" as the account name on some themes; it now reads the real
  one from the page header.
- **Litterbox** links are full-length again — omitting one optional field took a 6-character link.
- **TeraBytez** and **DataVaults** had no icon in the hoster list.

## Notes

- **DropGalaxy** and **ShareMods** remain present in the code but disabled, unchanged from 1.2.0.
